Chatting animation bridge
-------------------------
Sky VeLor contains an optional reflection bridge for the public configuration surface of a separately
installed compatible Chatting runtime. No Chatting runtime classes or binaries are bundled in this JAR.
When enabled by the player, the bridge limits that external runtime to its animation controls so Sky
VeLor's own optional channel tabs remain authoritative.
